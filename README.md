## Project Title
Analyzing what affects **students' grades**.

## Description
Me and a team of three of people analyzed which factors affect students' grades the most. Our data were of grades from two public high schools in Portugal during the 2006-2007 school year. Our goal was to discover which factors affected students' grades. Some factors included extracurriculars or study time. We executed descriptive analysis, regression analysis, data wrangling, hypothesis tests, correlations, and utilization of PivotTables. Also created <ins>visuals</ins> such as pie charts and bar charts to display our findings.
